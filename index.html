Activities

  • The Okutala Bush Table

    Come and dine with nature

    Leave the lodge behind and slow down beneath the open Namibian sky. Enjoy a relaxed bush dining experience surrounded by nature, with thoughtfully prepared food inspired by Namibia and time to savour the landscape, wildlife and quiet of the bush.

    TimingDetermined on arrival and tailored to individual guest preferences

  • The Okutala Etosha Expedition

    Discover the wider wilderness

    Set out from Okutala for a full day exploring the remarkable wildlife and landscapes of Etosha National Park. Accompanied by a knowledgeable guide, discover the park’s diverse wildlife, learn about the stories of the landscape and take time to observe animals in their natural surroundings. From the early morning departure to the journey back to Okutala, enjoy a day of discovery in one of Namibia’s most iconic wilderness destinations.

    Breakfast at the lodge07:00
    Depart from the lodge07:30
    Lunch at a restaurant in Etosha12:30 to 13:00 (dependent on weather and sightings)
    Return to the lodgeBetween 17:00 & 18:00
    • All trips are done on an open 10-seater Game Viewer vehicle
    • Water, juice, our signature coffee and some light snacks will be served
    • Ponchos are available on the vehicle
    • Kindly note that Etosha National Park is very dusty and hot during summer months. Protective gear is available for purchase at our curio shop the day before

    Half Day Etosha Expedition

    Due to the distance between the lodge and Etosha National Park, half day trips are not recommended. However, this can be arranged on special request.

  • The Okutala Bush Walk

    Come and walk with nature

    Leave the vehicle behind and discover Okutala on foot. Walk alongside a knowledgeable guide and learn to read the signs of the bush — from tracks and plants to birds, sounds and wildlife behaviour. Take your time, look closer and discover the details that make the Okutala landscape come alive.

    Breakfast at the lodge07:00
    Depart from the lodge07:30
    Return to the lodge10:00

  • The Okutala Waterhole

    See the wild from different perspectives

    The waterhole offers a place to slow down and observe the wildlife that visits Okutala throughout the day. Settle onto the main deck, take in the surrounding landscape and enjoy the anticipation of what might appear. The sunken hide is also available throughout the day, offering a unique, water-level perspective for quieter, more immersive wildlife observation. In the afternoon, visit the designated viewing area to experience the wildlife feeding. Whether watching from the deck, observing from the hide or enjoying the afternoon feeding, take a moment to look closer and experience the safari unfolding around you.

    The Main Deck overlooking the waterholeAvailable to guests the whole day
    Sunken Hide viewingAvailable to guests the whole day, guided by wildlife movement and safety considerations
    Afternoon Animal Feeding16:00

    Morning Safari at Okutala

    Begin your safari at Okutala. Set out in the early morning light to explore the wildlife and natural surroundings of the property with a knowledgeable guide. Take time to observe animal behaviour, discover tracks and smaller signs of life, and experience the bush as it awakens. Return to the lodge for a delicious breakfast.

    Depart from the lodge07:00
    Return to the lodge09:00

    Afternoon Safari at Okutala

    As the day begins to slow, discover a different side of Okutala. Explore the property with a knowledgeable guide, taking time to observe wildlife, interpret the landscape and enjoy the changing light. Pause at a scenic viewpoint for a refreshing drink and light snack before continuing your safari as the bush transitions into evening. Return to the lodge for a hearty dinner.

    Depart from the lodgeBetween 16:30 & 17:00

    When the sun goes down, the safari changes. Venture into the night with a knowledgeable guide and experience the bush through sounds, shadows and the search for nocturnal wildlife. Take time to observe animals in their nighttime world, listen to the changing sounds of the bush and pause beneath the Namibian night sky before returning to the lodge for a peaceful night’s rest.

    • Ponchos are available on the vehicle

Drive from Windhoek

Okutala Etosha is about 400km north of the capital city, Windhoek. From Windhoek take the B1 to Otjiwarongo then take the C38 to Etosha National Park’s Andersson Gate. From Outjo drive approximately 70km before turning left onto a gravel road D2695. Drive 6km on D2695, turn left at the Okutala Etosha main entrance gate. 11km on the gravel road will bring you to the main lodge where we will await you with our warm Okutala hospitality.

From Okutala Etosha Lodge to the Etosha Park Andersson gate is 40km.

Main Gate

Our Main Gate is manned by a Security guard 24 hours, seven days a week.

The gate is however locked between 19H00 and 07H00. Guests must sign in at arrival at the gate. The Guard will announce your arrival to Reception. If guests are delayed en route to us for whatsoever reason, they should call the lodge to make arrangements for late arrival. The guard is available to unlock the gate between 19H00 and 07H00 and allow guests entry to the farm.

Good to Know

Everything about your stay, from Wifi to laundry. Tap a topic for the details.

Banded Mongoose, Rhinos and Oubaas, our resident giraffe, roam freely through the lodge from time to time. The golden rules are:

  1. Keep your room doors closed to prevent the Mongoose entering your room
  2. Keep a safe distance between you and the animals, especially the Giraffe and Rhino
  3. Do not feed the animals
  4. BUT, take a lot of pictures

Two swimming pools are available, one at the Main Deck and one at Hilltop.

  • Guests swim at own risk
  • Children under 12 must be supervised by an adult
  • Diving or jumping in the pools is not allowed

Guests are not to use room towels for swimming. We supply different towels for swimming.

Due to the remote location of the lodge, the availability and strength of wi-fi cannot be guaranteed. We do provide free wi-fi at public areas:

  • Hilltop boma
  • Reception
  • Main deck

Guests are requested not to stream, download updates, send photos or videos.

Guests are requested to lock their rooms when not inside. Please close the doors to prevent small animals from entering. We provide a Night Porter service that is roaming the Lodge area to ensure all occupants are safe.

We do stock a variety of items in our curio shop. Reception staff will assist you.

Any outstanding payments for accommodation must be settled at Reception upon arrival. We have a single payment point at the lodge. All activities, drinks, meals and other services can be paid at Reception at check out. Card facilities are available.

Porters are on duty at Reception. They will move your luggage to and from your room. Please report to Reception if you require the Porter service upon check-out.

We have our own “Uber” service to transfer guests from Reception to Hilltop and back.

Please report to Reception for an Uber ride.

A lazy walk to and from Hilltop is always good for exercise.

Full Laundry service is available. Please use the basket and laundry list in the rooms, but also report to Reception if you require the service.
